                              Plan your first
                              Marswalk in Jezero

                              Momoot is a route planner for astronauts on Mars. The map is real NASA orbiter imagery, down to 25 cm per pixel. Pick today's tasks, let the planner find the best route, then drive it in the crew rover and walk out at every stop. Conditions on Mars change, so be ready to adapt.
