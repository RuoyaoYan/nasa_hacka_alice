                  Momoot is a route planner for astronauts on Mars. The map is real NASA orbiter imagery, down to 25 cm per pixel. Pick today's science tour, compare routes, then drive it in the crew rover and walk out at every stop. Conditions on Mars change, so be ready to adapt.
